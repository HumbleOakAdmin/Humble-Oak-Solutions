Operations

Where the work actually sticks

Most delays sit in a queue, a handoff, or a decision that waits on one person. The useful question is where material, orders, or information stop moving.

Start with one flow

Pick a product family or a job type that matters this quarter. Walk it from order to ship. Write the steps the way the floor does them, including the unofficial ones. Time the wait and the touch time.

If three people describe three different processes, the standard still needs to be written. That is the finding. Software comes after the process is agreed.

Name the constraint

The bottleneck is the step that sets the pace of the whole flow. Extra labour in front of it just builds WIP. Extra labour after it sits idle. Fix the constraint, then rebalance.

Write the new sequence down. Put a simple KPI on it: cycle time, on-time jobs, or WIP. Review it on a rhythm the team already has.

This is the same work we do in operations consulting. When those numbers still need to be built, business intelligence comes after the process is in place.
