After the builder

A finished company site is a set of files, a host, and a domain. Builders are convenient at the start. Years later the subscription is still there and exporting the site is limited. For a brochure, a static build covers the job: the same content, a host invoice you can see, and the source in Git.

After go-live we handle new pages, copy changes, form fields, redirects, certificate renewals, and DNS. Catalogues and large product sites are scoped as their own job.
