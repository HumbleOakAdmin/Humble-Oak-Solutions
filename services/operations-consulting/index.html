On the floor

Most of the hours are on the floor with the people who run the job. We time the queue, check WIP against what the ERP or the spreadsheet says, and sit in the planning meeting. The standard is then rewritten in the language the crew already uses.

The same work applies in manufacturing, warehousing, logistics, and production planning, including shops that live in an ERP. Past engagements have shown large productivity gains, shorter order cycles, and revenue movement. Those figures are above. The next scope is written from your floor.

Sequence

How it runs

  1. 01

    Current state

    One family or job type this quarter. Walk it from order to ship with the people who do the work, and write the unofficial steps down.

  2. 02

    Constraint

    The step that sets the pace is the one we redesign. Labour and inventory piled in front of it are the symptom.

  3. 03

    Standard and rhythm

    An SOP, a few KPIs, and a review the supervisor already has time to hold.
