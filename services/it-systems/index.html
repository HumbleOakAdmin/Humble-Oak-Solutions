Office and plant

Shop floors run on shared logins, kiosks, scanners, label printers, and engineering apps tied to the ERP. Those get the same administration as the front office, booked around the shift.

The weekly work is joiner-mover-leaver, licence cleanup, and PowerShell for the repeat admin. New staff get an account, a device, and the apps for the role. Departing staff lose access the same day. Server and network changes get coordinated with the vendor who owns that piece.

Sequence

How it runs

  1. 01

    Inventory

    Tenant, servers, endpoints, and floor devices, written down with an owner for each.

  2. 02

    Access and recovery

    MFA, groups, backups, and a restore test. Offboarding closes the account, the device, and the shared mailboxes.

  3. 03

    Runbook

    The steps for a new hire, a new printer, and an outage, so the next change follows a procedure.
