Business Intelligence

Power BI and Excel reporting tied back to the system the number came from, so the Monday meeting uses one definition.

Home/Services/Business Intelligence

We write the measure first: what is included, what is excluded, and which system it ties back to. The visual comes after the total matches that system. Production, inventory, purchasing, quality, and revenue are the usual subjects.

The engagement

For
Owners and managers who need one version of the weekly numbers
Tools
Power BI, Power Query, DAX, and Excel
You keep
A model, a refresh schedule, and a named owner for each measure

Scope

The work

  1. 01

    Data model, Power Query steps, and DAX measures

  2. 02

    One-page operating and management reports

  3. 03

    KPI definitions matched to how the plant or the books run

  4. 04

    Excel and Power Query when a full model is more than the question needs

  5. 05

    Tie-out to the ERP, the inventory system, or the source workbook

Before the chart

We agree the source of record before the model is built. Grain, relationships, and measures come next. The report total is checked against the ERP or the workbook of record, and then the page is designed.

Manufacturing reports usually cover output, WIP, purchasing, quality, or sales. Each page has a refresh time and a person who answers when a figure looks off.

Sequence

How it runs

  1. 01

    Definition

    The decision the report serves, in one sentence, with the exclusions written down.

  2. 02

    Tie-out

    Totals checked to the ERP or the workbook of record before the report is shared.

  3. 03

    Publish

    A page people open in the weekly meeting, a refresh schedule, and a name beside the measure.
